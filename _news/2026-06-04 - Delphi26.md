New Preprint Available: <b><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04490" target="_blank">Prioritization of Risks from Artificial Intelligence: A Delphi Study of 272 International Experts</a></b>. A three-round Delphi study with 272 international AI experts rating the probability, severity, and responsibility for 24 AI risks. Joint work with <a href="https://aksaeri.com/" target="_blank">Alexander K. Saeri</a> and 270 other international AI safety and policy experts, coordinated through the <a href="https://airisk.mit.edu/priorities" target="_blank">MIT AI Risk Repository</a>.
