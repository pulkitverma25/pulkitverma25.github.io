BIO

I have completed M.Tech. from Department of Computer Science & Engineering at IIT Guwahati, advised by Dr. Pradip K. Das. I received a B.E. in Information Technology from Shri Vaishnav Institute of Technology and Science, Indore, affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal.

I currently work as Member of Technical Staff at Netapp Inc., Bengaluru, India. I also contribute to Stanford Crowd Course Initiative and Stanford Scholar run by Rajan Vaish. My primary interests lies in Speech Processing, Machine Learning and Operating Systems.
